# Creative

PURPOSE: reality-bending transformations of the scene (underwater, fire, ice, glass, overgrown, zero-g, mirror, miniature)
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants to dramatically transform the world/physics of the scene, not just stylize it
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, cinematic-styles, depth, framing, lenses, emotions, weather (sometimes), cameras
CONFLICTS WITH: commercial-styles (commercial shots want plausible reality), film-stocks (they describe a real photograph), realism (creative intentionally breaks reality)

NOTE: typically use ONE creative tool per image. Stacking (e.g. underwater + fire) usually fails or muddies the look.

---

## CREATIVE-UNDERWATER — Underwater

Transform this scene as if it were underwater. Add caustic light patterns, floating particles, subtle blue-green color cast, bubbles, and the distorted quality of light through water. Objects drift slightly, hair and fabric float.

---

## CREATIVE-FIRE — Ablaze

Set this scene ablaze. Add dramatic flames, glowing embers, smoke, and intense orange-red lighting. Fire reflects off surfaces, shadows dance. The atmosphere is thick with heat haze and ash particles.

---

## CREATIVE-ICE — Frozen In Ice

Freeze this scene in ice. Cover surfaces with frost and ice crystals. Add frozen breath vapor, icicles, and a cold blue color cast. Everything looks preserved in a moment of extreme cold.

---

## CREATIVE-GLASS — Glass Forms

Transform everything in this scene to be made of transparent glass. Show light refracting through glass forms, reflections, and the fragile crystalline quality of glass objects. Maintain recognizable shapes.

---

## CREATIVE-OVERGROWN — Nature Reclaimed

Let nature reclaim this scene. Add vines, moss, plants growing through cracks, flowers blooming unexpectedly. Years of abandonment, but beautiful decay. Green overtakes everything.

---

## CREATIVE-ZERO-GRAVITY — Zero Gravity

Remove gravity from this scene. Objects float and drift, hair and fabric billow upward, liquids form spheres, papers and small items hover. The weightless moment frozen in time.

---

## CREATIVE-MIRROR-DIMENSION — Mirror Dimension

Flip this scene into a mirror dimension. Everything is reflected and reversed, with an otherworldly quality. Add subtle visual distortions, impossible reflections, and a dreamlike atmosphere.

---

## CREATIVE-MINIATURE — Miniature Toys

Transform this scene to look like miniature toys or a diorama. Add tilt-shift blur, make everything look like tiny models. Plastic-like textures, visible seams, the charming imperfection of handmade miniatures.
