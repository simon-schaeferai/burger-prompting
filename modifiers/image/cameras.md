# Cameras

PURPOSE: defines the capture device and its characteristic look (sensor, processing, format)
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants the image to look like it was shot on a specific named camera or device
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, depth, framing, emotions, weather, realism, lighting (some), lenses (only the cameras that don't define their own optics)
CONFLICTS WITH: film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, photo-effects (all compete for the overall look). Some cameras also lock in their lens character — see notes.

NOTES:
- CAM-POLAROID is a near-duplicate of FILM-POLAROID-600 (camera vs film angle of the same look). Pick ONE.
- Cameras that LOCK their lens (do not stack with lenses category): CAM-GOPRO, CAM-IPHONE-15-PRO, CAM-DISPOSABLE, CAM-CCTV, CAM-WEBCAM, CAM-VHS, CAM-RING, CAM-DJI-DRONE
- Cameras that LEAVE room for lens choice: CAM-ARRI-ALEXA-35, CAM-LEICA-M11, CAM-POLAROID (loosely)

---

## CAM-ARRI-ALEXA-35 — ARRI Alexa 35

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was shot on an ARRI Alexa 35 cinema camera. Exceptional dynamic range, natural organic color science, beautiful skin tones. Film-like texture with subtle grain. Hollywood A-list production value.

---

## CAM-IPHONE-15-PRO — iPhone 15 Pro

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was shot on an iPhone 15 Pro. Computational photography, aggressive HDR processing. Over-sharpened details, punchy saturated colors. Small sensor depth of field, everything in focus. Social media optimized smartphone aesthetic.

---

## CAM-GOPRO — GoPro Hero 12

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was shot on a GoPro Hero 12. Ultra-wide 170° fisheye distortion, barrel warping at edges. Vibrant action cam colors, first-person POV, extreme sports aesthetic.

---

## CAM-DJI-DRONE — DJI Drone

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was shot on a DJI drone. Aerial perspective, wide angle lens, deep focus throughout. Stabilized smooth look, travel video aesthetic.

---

## CAM-DISPOSABLE — Disposable Camera

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was shot on a disposable camera. Built-in flash harsh frontal light, red-eye effect. Heavy vignetting, light leaks, unpredictable color shifts. Grainy low-quality film, amateur snapshot aesthetic.

---

## CAM-POLAROID — Polaroid Instant Camera

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was shot on a Polaroid instant camera. Square format, white border frame. Faded pastel colors, soft dreamy quality. Slight blur, imperfect focus, nostalgic analog aesthetic.

---

## CAM-CCTV — Security Camera

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was captured on a security camera CCTV. Fixed wide angle, ceiling-mounted perspective. Low resolution, compression artifacts. Timestamp overlay in corner, black and white or green night vision tint.

---

## CAM-WEBCAM — Cheap Webcam

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was captured on a cheap 720p webcam. Low resolution, heavy compression artifacts. Washed out colors, noisy, autofocus hunting. Video call aesthetic, amateur livestream quality.

---

## CAM-VHS — VHS Camcorder

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was recorded on a VHS camcorder. Analog video noise, tracking lines, color bleeding. Low resolution softness, rainbow fringing. Date stamp in corner, 1980s-90s home video aesthetic.

---

## CAM-LEICA-M11 — Leica M11 Rangefinder

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was shot on a Leica M11 rangefinder. Classic Leica color science, subtle warm tones. Beautiful bokeh, street photography aesthetic. Timeless documentary look.

---

## CAM-RING — Ring Doorbell

Change the style of this image completely. It should look like it was captured on a Ring doorbell camera. Extreme wide angle fisheye, porch-level perspective. HDR processing, slightly overexposed. Low resolution, motion-triggered recording aesthetic.
