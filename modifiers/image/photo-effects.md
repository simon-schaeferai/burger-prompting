# Photo Effects

PURPOSE: post-process style effects applied to the image (motion blur, B&W treatments, color shifts, lab tricks)
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants a specific photographic technique or processing effect
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, depth, framing, emotions, weather, realism
CONFLICTS WITH: cinematic-styles, film-stocks, commercial-styles, cameras (all compete for the overall look). Some effects also conflict internally — see notes below.

NOTES ON INTERNAL CONFLICTS:
- EFFECT-CRUSHED-BLACKS conflicts with EFFECT-LIFTED-BLACKS
- B&W effects (EFFECT-BW-HIGH-CONTRAST, EFFECT-FILM-NOIR-BW, EFFECT-CYANOTYPE, EFFECT-DUOTONE, EFFECT-INFRARED) conflict with each other and with most color-shift effects
- EFFECT-SILHOUETTE is greedy — overrides most other effects on the subject

---

## EFFECT-MOTION-BLUR — Motion Blur

Add motion blur to this image. Subject shows dynamic movement with motion blur streaks. Sense of speed and action. Shutter drag effect with intentional blur on moving elements while some parts remain sharp. Kinetic energy frozen in motion.

---

## EFFECT-STREET-PHOTO — Classic Street Photography

Transform to classic street photography style. Candid urban moment, decisive instant captured. High contrast black and white or gritty color. Raw authentic documentary feel. Imperfect framing adds to the spontaneity. Henri Cartier-Bresson or Vivian Maier aesthetic.

---

## EFFECT-BW-HIGH-CONTRAST — High Contrast Black And White

Convert to high contrast black and white. Deep rich blacks, bright clean whites. Strong graphic shapes, dramatic shadows. No middle grays - stark tonal separation. Classic fine art monochrome.

---

## EFFECT-FILM-NOIR-BW — Film Noir Black And White

Convert to film noir black and white. Venetian blind shadows, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting. Mystery and intrigue atmosphere. Low key with pools of light. 1940s Hollywood crime drama aesthetic.

---

## EFFECT-DOCUMENTARY — Documentary

Transform to documentary photography style. Raw, unposed, authentic moment. Available light, no flash. Candid expressions, real environments. Photojournalistic integrity, no manipulation. Truth in the frame.

---

## EFFECT-DOUBLE-EXPOSURE — Double Exposure

Create a double exposure effect. Two overlapping images blended together. Subject merged with nature, cityscape, or abstract pattern. Ethereal layered composition. Surreal artistic effect.

---

## EFFECT-LONG-EXPOSURE — Long Exposure

Apply long exposure effect. Light trails from moving sources. Silky smooth water or clouds. Static elements sharp, motion shows as blur streaks. Time compressed into single frame.

---

## EFFECT-TILT-SHIFT — Tilt-Shift Miniature

Apply tilt-shift miniature effect. Selective focus creates toy-like appearance. Strong blur at top and bottom, sharp band in middle. Saturated colors, high contrast. Makes real scene look like a diorama.

---

## EFFECT-SILHOUETTE — Dramatic Silhouette

Transform to dramatic silhouette. Subject completely dark against bright background. Strong backlight, no detail in subject. Shape and outline only. Graphic simplicity, high contrast.

---

## EFFECT-REFLECTIONS — Reflections

Add or emphasize reflections. Mirror-like surface reflecting the scene. Water, glass, or polished floor. Symmetrical or distorted reflection. Dreamy doubled world.

---

## EFFECT-LENS-FLARE — Lens Flare And Haze

Add lens flare and atmospheric haze. Sun catching the lens, hexagonal artifacts. Dreamy soft haze reducing contrast. Golden light bleeding into shadows. Romantic ethereal mood.

---

## EFFECT-CRUSHED-BLACKS — Crushed Blacks

Apply crushed blacks effect. Shadow detail eliminated, pure black shadows. High contrast with lost information in darks. Moody dramatic atmosphere. Fashion editorial aesthetic.

---

## EFFECT-LIFTED-BLACKS — Lifted Blacks

Apply lifted blacks effect. Shadows raised to dark gray, no true black. Soft flat vintage feel. Faded matte finish. Instagram-style fade aesthetic.

---

## EFFECT-CROSS-PROCESS — Cross-Processing

Apply cross-processing effect. Film processed in wrong chemicals. Shifted colors - cyan shadows, yellow highlights. High contrast, unusual color cast. Experimental lab accident aesthetic.

---

## EFFECT-CHROMATIC-ABERRATION — Chromatic Aberration

Add chromatic aberration effect. Color fringing on edges, RGB channel separation. Glitchy lo-fi aesthetic. Red/cyan color split on high contrast areas. Imperfect lens effect.

---

## EFFECT-DUOTONE — Duotone

Apply duotone color effect. Image reduced to two colors only. Classic combination like blue/orange or teal/magenta. Graphic poster aesthetic. Bold simplified palette.

---

## EFFECT-INFRARED — Infrared

Apply infrared photography effect. Foliage glows white, sky goes dark. False color rendering. Surreal otherworldly landscape. Invisible light spectrum aesthetic.

---

## EFFECT-CYANOTYPE — Cyanotype Blueprint

Apply cyanotype blueprint effect. Prussian blue and white only. Historic photographic process look. High contrast, no middle tones. Alternative process fine art aesthetic.

---

## EFFECT-SPLIT-TONING — Split Toning

Apply split toning effect. Warm tones in highlights, cool tones in shadows. Or vice versa. Color contrast between light and dark. Professional color grading technique.

---

## EFFECT-BLEACH-BYPASS — Bleach Bypass

Apply bleach bypass effect. Desaturated with high contrast. Silver retained in film process. Gritty war movie aesthetic. Muted colors with metallic sheen. Saving Private Ryan look.
