# Lenses

PURPOSE: defines lens character (bokeh, flare, sharpness falloff, distortion, halation)
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants a specific optical signature applied to the rendering
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, depth, framing, emotions, weather, realism, film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, cameras (the ones that don't lock their own optics)
CONFLICTS WITH: cameras that already define their own lens character (see cameras.md notes). Photo-effects that fight the lens look (EFFECT-CHROMATIC-ABERRATION can clash with clean lens choices).

NOTE: use ONE lens per image.

---

## LENS-PETZVAL — Petzval Swirl

Apply Petzval lens characteristics. Subject in center is tack sharp. Background only has dramatic swirly bokeh - oval highlights spinning in circular motion behind the subject. Strong vignetting darkening the edges. The swirl effect is exclusively in the out-of-focus background, not on the subject. Vintage dreamy romantic quality.

---

## LENS-HELIOS-44-2 — Helios 44-2

Apply Helios 44-2 Soviet lens characteristics. Signature swirly bokeh with circular motion in out-of-focus areas. Soft dreamy rendering, slight glow on highlights. Warm color cast, lower contrast. Vintage Russian cinema look. Budget Petzval alternative aesthetic.

---

## LENS-COOKE — Cooke Speed Panchro

Apply Cooke Speed Panchro vintage cine lens look. Creamy smooth bokeh, gentle focus falloff. Warm skin tone rendering, subtle halation on highlights. Low contrast, filmic softness. Classic Hollywood golden age cinematography. The 'Cooke Look' with organic character.

---

## LENS-ANAMORPHIC — Anamorphic

Apply anamorphic lens characteristics. Single horizontal blue lens flare streak across the center of frame. Oval bokeh shapes, not circular. 2.39:1 widescreen aspect feel. Subtle edge distortion. Cinematic blockbuster look with one distinctive centered flare.

---

## LENS-SUMMILUX — Leica Summilux

Apply Leica Summilux lens rendering. Razor sharp center with smooth creamy bokeh transition. Subtle glow wide open, clinical sharpness stopped down. Neutral color science, micro-contrast excellence. German optical precision with 3D pop. Premium rangefinder aesthetic.

---

## LENS-ZEISS-PLANAR — Zeiss Planar

Apply vintage Zeiss Planar lens characteristics. Contrasty rendering with punchy colors. Sharp across frame, controlled chromatic aberration. Classic Zeiss 3D pop and subject separation. German precision with clinical accuracy. Medium format cinema quality.

---

## LENS-DREAM-LENS — Canon 50mm f/0.95 Dream Lens

Apply Canon 50mm f/0.95 Dream Lens characteristics. Extremely shallow depth of field, razor thin focus plane. Ethereal glow and halation wide open. Soft dreamy rendering, low contrast. Magical light bleeding. The ultimate portrait lens look.

---

## LENS-LENSBABY — Lensbaby Selective Focus

Apply Lensbaby selective focus effect. One sharp sweet spot surrounded by progressive blur. Tilt-shift like plane of focus. Creative blur zone, dreamy edges. Artistic selective focus, toy camera meets professional. Unique compositional emphasis.
