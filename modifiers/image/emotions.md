# Emotions

PURPOSE: sets the emotional expression of a character (face, body language, posture)
WHEN TO LOAD: when there is a character in the scene and the user wants to define or change their emotional state
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, photo-effects, depth, framing, lenses, weather, realism, cameras
CONFLICTS WITH: nothing categorically. Note: applies only when a character/face is in the scene. Pick ONE emotion per character.

---

## EMO-JOY — Pure Joy

Change the character's expression to pure joy. Genuine smile reaching the eyes, crow's feet visible. Raised cheeks, open relaxed face. Bright eyes full of warmth. Body language open and welcoming.

---

## EMO-SADNESS — Deep Sadness

Change the character's expression to deep sadness. Downcast eyes, slightly furrowed brow. Corners of mouth turned down, slumped shoulders. Withdrawn posture, gaze lowered. Visible emotional weight.

---

## EMO-ANGER — Intense Anger

Change the character's expression to intense anger. Furrowed brow, narrowed eyes, flared nostrils. Clenched jaw, tense neck muscles. Aggressive forward posture, clenched fists. Controlled rage visible.

---

## EMO-FEAR — Fear

Change the character's expression to fear. Wide eyes showing whites, raised eyebrows. Tense frozen posture, shoulders raised defensively. Mouth slightly open, shallow breathing visible. Ready to flee.

---

## EMO-SURPRISE — Genuine Surprise

Change the character's expression to genuine surprise. Wide open eyes, raised eyebrows high. Open mouth, head pulled back slightly. Frozen mid-motion, caught off guard. Split-second of shock.

---

## EMO-DISGUST — Disgust

Change the character's expression to disgust. Wrinkled nose, raised upper lip. Head turned slightly away, chin pulled back. Narrowed eyes, recoiling posture. Visceral repulsion visible.

---

## EMO-CONTEMPT — Contempt

Change the character's expression to contempt. One-sided smirk, raised corner of mouth. Slightly raised chin, looking down. Cold dismissive eyes, superior posture. Subtle disdain.

---

## EMO-LONGING — Deep Longing

Change the character's expression to deep longing. Soft distant gaze, slightly parted lips. Wistful expression, gentle sadness in eyes. Reaching or leaning toward something unseen. Quiet yearning.

---

## EMO-DETERMINATION — Fierce Determination

Change the character's expression to fierce determination. Set jaw, focused intense eyes. Slight forward lean, squared shoulders. Unwavering gaze, inner strength visible. Ready for anything.

---

## EMO-EXHAUSTION — Complete Exhaustion

Change the character's expression to complete exhaustion. Heavy-lidded eyes, slack facial muscles. Slumped posture, head slightly bowed. Visible fatigue, drained of energy. Running on empty.

---

## EMO-MISCHIEF — Playful Mischief

Change the character's expression to playful mischief. Sly grin, raised eyebrow. Glint in the eye, knowing look. Slightly tilted head, suppressed smile. Up to something fun.

---

## EMO-VULNERABILITY — Raw Vulnerability

Change the character's expression to raw vulnerability. Soft open eyes, unguarded expression. Slightly trembling lip, exposed emotion. Defenses down, authentic and fragile. Deeply human moment.

---

## EMO-PRIDE — Quiet Pride

Change the character's expression to quiet pride. Chin slightly raised, confident smile. Chest expanded, shoulders back. Warm satisfied eyes, dignified posture. Earned accomplishment.

---

## EMO-CURIOSITY — Intense Curiosity

Change the character's expression to intense curiosity. Slightly tilted head, raised eyebrows. Wide attentive eyes, leaning forward. Focused concentration, eager to understand. Mind actively engaged.

---

## EMO-BOREDOM — Utter Boredom

Change the character's expression to utter boredom. Glazed unfocused eyes, slack expression. Head resting on hand, slumped posture. Subtle eye roll, complete disinterest. Waiting for something to happen.

---

## EMO-SERENITY — Complete Serenity

Change the character's expression to complete serenity. Soft closed or half-lidded eyes, gentle smile. Relaxed facial muscles, peaceful countenance. Slow breathing, total inner calm. At peace.
