# Scene Extender

PURPOSE: extends the canvas outward (left, right, up, down) or zooms out to reveal more of the scene
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants to see MORE of the environment beyond the current frame edges
STACKS WITH: lighting, film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, photo-effects, realism, weather, emotions
CONFLICTS WITH: shots, angles, framing, single-shots (all redefine composition rather than extending it)

NOTE: directional extends (left/right/up/down) can be combined sequentially. Zoom-out variants should be used alone, not stacked with each other.

---

## EXTEND-LEFT — Extend Left

Extend the scene to the left. Reveal what is beyond the left edge of the frame. Continue the environment, lighting, and atmosphere seamlessly. Show more of the world in that direction.

---

## EXTEND-RIGHT — Extend Right

Extend the scene to the right. Reveal what is beyond the right edge of the frame. Continue the environment, lighting, and atmosphere seamlessly. Show more of the world in that direction.

---

## EXTEND-UP — Extend Upward

Extend the scene upward. Reveal what is above the top edge of the frame. Continue the environment, show the sky, ceiling, or upper architecture. Maintain consistent lighting and atmosphere.

---

## EXTEND-DOWN — Extend Downward

Extend the scene downward. Reveal what is below the bottom edge of the frame. Show the ground, floor, or lower environment. Maintain consistent lighting and atmosphere.

---

## EXTEND-ZOOM-2X — Zoom Out 2x

Zoom out to show twice as much of the scene. The current image becomes the center of a larger view. Reveal the surrounding environment on all sides. Maintain style and lighting consistency.

---

## EXTEND-ZOOM-4X — Zoom Out 4x

Zoom out dramatically to show four times as much of the scene. The current image becomes a small part of a much larger view. Reveal the full environment and context. Maintain style and lighting.
