# Weather

PURPOSE: adds weather elements (snow, rain, fog) to the scene
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants to change atmospheric conditions
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, depth, framing, emotions, lenses, realism, film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, photo-effects, cameras, creative
CONFLICTS WITH: nothing categorically. Internal and cross-category conflicts below.

INTERNAL CONFLICTS:
- WEATHER-SNOW and WEATHER-RAIN do not stack (different temperatures)
- WEATHER-FOG can layer with WEATHER-SNOW or WEATHER-RAIN (cold + misty), but pick carefully

CROSS-CATEGORY NOTES:
- WEATHER-SNOW / WEATHER-RAIN / WEATHER-FOG fight LIGHT-HARD-SUN, LIGHT-GOLDEN-HOUR, LIGHT-DAY (clear-weather lighting)
- WEATHER-RAIN pairs naturally with STYLE-NEON-NOIR, LIGHT-NIGHT, LIGHT-NEON
- WEATHER-FOG pairs naturally with LIGHT-GOD-RAYS, DEPTH-AERIAL

---

## WEATHER-SNOW — Snow

Add snow to this scene. Fresh white snow on all surfaces, snowflakes in the air, frost on edges. Cold blue-white color temperature. Winter atmosphere. Keep everything else intact.

---

## WEATHER-RAIN — Rain

Add rain to this scene. Raindrops falling, wet reflective surfaces, puddles where appropriate. Overcast lighting, glistening wet textures. Moody atmosphere. Keep all elements but make them wet.

---

## WEATHER-FOG — Thick Fog

Add thick fog to this scene. Reduced visibility, objects fading into mist. Soft diffused lighting, no harsh shadows. Foreground sharp, background disappearing into haze. Mysterious atmosphere.
