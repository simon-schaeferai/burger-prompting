# Depth

PURPOSE: adds spatial layers (foreground/midground/background) and atmospheric depth cues
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants more dimensionality, bokeh, atmospheric haze, or layered foreground/midground elements
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, photo-effects, framing, realism, lenses, emotions, weather
CONFLICTS WITH: nothing categorically. Note: DEPTH-FG-FRAME overlaps semantically with the framing category — pick one or the other, not both.

---

## DEPTH-FG-BLUR — Foreground Blur Element

Add a soft, out-of-focus element in the immediate foreground. Choose something that fits naturally in this scene and makes sense for the environment. Blurred shape partially visible at the edge of frame. Creates depth separation.

---

## DEPTH-FG-FRAME — Foreground Framing Element

Add an element in the foreground that partially frames the scene. Choose something appropriate for this environment that makes sense contextually. Creates a natural vignette and depth layering.

---

## DEPTH-AERIAL — Atmospheric / Aerial Perspective

Add atmospheric depth to the background that fits the scene naturally. Distant elements become hazier, slightly desaturated, with reduced contrast. Aerial perspective creates sense of distance.

---

## DEPTH-BOKEH — Background Bokeh

Transform the background into beautiful bokeh with light sources that make sense for this scene. Soft, circular out-of-focus light orbs appropriate to the environment. Subject pops against the dreamy blurred backdrop.

---

## DEPTH-MIDGROUND — Midground Element

Add an element in the midground between subject and background. Choose something that belongs naturally in this scene and makes contextual sense. Slightly softer focus than main subject.

---

## DEPTH-PARTICLES — Floating Particles

Add floating particles in the air that fit naturally in this scene. Choose particles appropriate for the environment and context. Visible at different distances showing depth through focus variation.
