# Shots

PURPOSE: controls framing scale, how much of the subject and environment fills the frame
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants to change shot size, zoom, or how tight/wide the framing is
STACKS WITH: angles, lenses, lighting, film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, depth, framing, realism, emotions, weather
CONFLICTS WITH: single-shots, scene-extender (both reframe the existing image and override shot choice)

---

## SHOT-EWS — Extreme Wide Shot

Change the shot size to an extreme wide shot. Vast environment dominates, main subject is small in frame. Epic scale, establishes location.

---

## SHOT-FULL — Full Shot

Change the shot size to a full shot. Complete subject visible from top to bottom. Shows full form and scale.

---

## SHOT-CU — Close-Up

Change the shot size to a close-up. Subject fills the frame. Emphasis on detail, texture, and fine features.

---

## SHOT-MACRO — Macro Shot

Change the shot size to a macro shot. Extreme magnification revealing details invisible to naked eye. Texture and micro-detail.

---

## SHOT-WIDE — Wide Shot

Change the shot size to a wide shot. Full environment visible with subject in context. Shows spatial relationships and setting.

---

## SHOT-MS — Medium Shot

Change the shot size to a medium shot. Subject fills roughly half the frame. Good balance of detail and context.

---

## SHOT-BCU — Big Close-Up

Change the shot size to a big close-up. Tight framing on key details. Intense focus, intimate view.

---

## SHOT-INSERT — Insert Shot

Change the shot size to an insert shot. Close-up of specific detail relevant to the scene. Draws attention to important element.

---

## SHOT-MWS — Medium Wide Shot

Change the shot size to a medium wide shot. Subject with surrounding context visible. Balance of subject and environment.

---

## SHOT-MCU — Medium Close-Up

Change the shot size to a medium close-up. Subject fills most of frame with some context. Focus shifts to details.

---

## SHOT-ECU — Extreme Close-Up

Change the shot size to an extreme close-up. Single detail or feature fills entire frame. Abstract level of detail.

---

## SHOT-DETAIL — Detail Shot

Change the shot size to a detail shot. Isolated view of a specific part or feature. Removes context, pure focus on the detail.
