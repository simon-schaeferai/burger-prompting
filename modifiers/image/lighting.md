# Lighting

PURPOSE: defines the lighting setup (time of day, light quality, direction, color, contrast ratio)
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants to change the light source, mood, or time of day
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, depth, framing, emotions, lenses, realism, weather (some combos), film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, photo-effects, cameras, single-shots, scene-extender, creative
CONFLICTS WITH: nothing categorically — but pick ONE lighting setup per image. Internal conflicts below.

INTERNAL CONFLICTS:
- LIGHT-DAY vs LIGHT-NIGHT
- LIGHT-HIGH-KEY vs LIGHT-LOW-KEY
- LIGHT-HARD-SUN vs LIGHT-SOFT-DAYLIGHT / LIGHT-WINDOW
- LIGHT-GOLDEN-HOUR vs midday lighting
- LIGHT-NEON typically implies night, conflicts with LIGHT-DAY / LIGHT-GOLDEN-HOUR / LIGHT-HARD-SUN

STACKING NOTES:
- LIGHT-RIM stacks well as an additional layer on top of most base lighting setups
- LIGHT-GOD-RAYS is an atmospheric addition, compatible with most base setups that include haze

---

## LIGHT-NIGHT — Nighttime

Nighttime. Dark environment, artificial light sources or ambient night lighting as primary illumination. Deep shadows, reduced visibility in distance.

---

## LIGHT-DAY — Daytime

Daytime. Bright natural daylight, sun as primary light source. Clear visibility, natural outdoor illumination. Blue sky ambient light.

---

## LIGHT-SOFT-DAYLIGHT — Soft Natural Daylight

Change the lighting to soft natural daylight. Remove all hard light sources and harsh shadows. Single soft diffused light source from the left side. Gentle shadow gradients, no sharp edges. Neutral color temperature. Like a large window or overcast sky from camera left.

---

## LIGHT-GOLDEN-HOUR — Golden Hour

Change the lighting to golden hour. Warm 3000K color temperature, soft directional sun at low angle, rich amber and orange tones, long dramatic shadows, magic hour atmosphere.

---

## LIGHT-RIM — Dramatic Rim Lighting

Change the lighting to dramatic rim lighting. Strong backlight creating luminous edge glow, subject separated from dark background, hair light effect, professional studio separation.

---

## LIGHT-WINDOW — Soft Window Light

Change the lighting to soft window light. Diffused natural illumination from one side, gentle wraparound shadows, Rembrandt lighting triangle, flattering and natural.

---

## LIGHT-HIGH-KEY — High-Key

Change the lighting to high-key setup. Bright overexposed background, minimal shadows, ethereal airy feel, dreamy optimistic mood, fashion editorial style.

---

## LIGHT-LOW-KEY — Low-Key Dramatic

Change the lighting to low-key dramatic. Deep shadows dominating frame, single focused light source, high contrast ratio, film noir aesthetic, mysterious moody atmosphere.

---

## LIGHT-HARD-SUN — Hard Directional Sunlight

Change the lighting to hard directional sunlight. Noon sun 5600K, crisp defined shadows, high contrast, intense highlights, dramatic modeling, strong three-dimensional depth.

---

## LIGHT-NEON — Vibrant Neon

Change the lighting to vibrant neon. Hot pink and electric cyan colored light, cyberpunk color palette, strong color contrast, glowing reflections, futuristic atmosphere.

---

## LIGHT-GOD-RAYS — Volumetric God Rays

Change the lighting to include volumetric god rays. Atmospheric haze particles, visible light beams cutting through mist, crepuscular rays, mystical atmospheric quality.
