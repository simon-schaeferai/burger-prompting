# Film Stocks

PURPOSE: defines the chemical film look (color science, grain structure, contrast, halation)
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants a specific analog film aesthetic by name
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, weather, depth, framing, emotions, realism
CONFLICTS WITH: cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, cameras, photo-effects (all compete for the overall look)

---

## FILM-PORTRA-400 — Kodak Portra 400

Change the image to be shot on Kodak Portra 400 film stock. Soft pastel color palette with muted tones. Creamy skin rendering with subtle warmth. Visible authentic film grain throughout the image. Lifted shadows, gentle highlight rolloff. Natural daylight balanced, slightly desaturated greens and blues.

---

## FILM-KODACHROME-64 — Kodachrome 64

Change the image to be shot on Kodachrome 64 slide film. Legendary rich saturated colors with strong red-cyan contrast. Deep inky blacks, punchy midtones. Warm golden skin tones, vivid greens. Visible film grain texture. Archival 1960s-70s documentary look.

---

## FILM-CINESTILL-800T — Cinestill 800T

Change the image to be shot on Cinestill 800T tungsten film. Strong cyan-teal shadows, warm orange highlights. Distinctive red halation glow around bright lights. Heavy visible high ISO grain structure throughout. Cinematic night photography aesthetic.

---

## FILM-TRI-X — Kodak Tri-X 400

Change the image to be shot on Kodak Tri-X 400 black and white film. Iconic American photojournalism look. Punchy contrast, heavy gritty visible grain throughout the entire image. Deep shadows with detail, bright specular highlights. Raw 1960s street photography aesthetic.

---

## FILM-VELVIA-50 — Fujichrome Velvia 50

Change the image to be shot on Fujichrome Velvia 50 slide film. Hyper-saturated vivid colors, especially reds and greens. Fine but visible film grain texture. Extreme sharpness. High contrast with crushed shadows. Nature documentary vibrancy.

---

## FILM-POLAROID-600 — Polaroid 600

Change the image to be shot on Polaroid 600 instant film. Soft dreamy focus with edge falloff. Faded vintage colors with cyan-yellow shift. Creamy overexposed highlights, muddy shadows. Visible grain and chemical texture. Nostalgic instant photo imperfection.
