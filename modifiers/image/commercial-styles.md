# Commercial Styles

PURPOSE: defines a commercial/brand photography genre (e-commerce, editorial, lifestyle, etc.)
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants a specific commercial application or brand aesthetic
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, lenses, depth, framing, realism, emotions
CONFLICTS WITH: cinematic-styles, film-stocks, photo-effects, cameras (all compete for the overall look)

---

## COMM-ECOMMERCE — Clean E-Commerce

Change the image to be clean e-commerce product photography, seamless white backdrop, professional retouch, even studio lighting, crisp detail, commercial quality, perfect focus.

---

## COMM-EDITORIAL-FASHION — High-End Editorial Fashion

Change the image to be high-end editorial fashion photography, crisp defined shadows, neutral accurate skin tones, dramatic yet elegant lighting, sophisticated composition, premium quality.

---

## COMM-LIFESTYLE — Lifestyle Brand

Change the image to have lifestyle brand aesthetic, natural candid moment, warm inviting tones, authentic atmosphere, aspirational yet relatable, soft natural lighting.

---

## COMM-LUXURY — Luxury Product

Change the image to luxury product photography. Dark moody backdrop, dramatic accent lighting. Rich deep shadows, selective highlights on product. Premium high-end feel, expensive materials emphasized.

---

## COMM-FOOD — Professional Food

Change the image to professional food photography style. Appetizing warm tones, soft directional light from side. Fresh vibrant colors, shallow depth of field. Hero angle, mouth-watering presentation.

---

## COMM-REAL-ESTATE — Real Estate

Change the image to real estate photography style. Bright airy interiors, balanced exposure throughout. Wide angle but minimal distortion. Blue sky through windows, inviting warm interior tones.

---

## COMM-BEAUTY — Beauty Campaign

Change the image to beauty campaign aesthetic. Flawless skin with visible texture, not plastic. Soft beauty lighting, catchlights in eyes. Clean backdrop, focus on skin and features. High-end cosmetic brand quality.

---

## COMM-TECH — Tech Product

Change the image to tech product photography. Clean minimal backdrop, precise studio lighting. Sharp reflections on surfaces, crisp edges. Modern sleek aesthetic, premium device presentation.

---

## COMM-AUTOMOTIVE — Automotive

Change the image to automotive photography style. Dramatic lighting emphasizing curves and lines. Reflective surfaces showing environment. Dynamic angle, powerful presence. Showroom or location quality.

---

## COMM-TRAVEL — Travel

Change the image to travel photography aesthetic. Vibrant saturated colors, golden hour warmth. Aspirational wanderlust feel, inviting destinations. Clear blue skies, lifestyle integration.

---

## COMM-CORPORATE — Corporate Portrait

Change the image to corporate portrait style. Professional clean backdrop, even flattering light. Confident approachable expression, business appropriate. LinkedIn and website headshot quality.
