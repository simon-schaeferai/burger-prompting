# Framing

PURPOSE: recomposes the scene with a frame-within-the-frame (doorway, window, mirror, foliage, etc.)
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants the scene viewed through or framed by something
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, photo-effects, depth, realism, lenses, emotions, weather
CONFLICTS WITH: scene-extender (extending the canvas outward fights with framing the existing edge), single-shots (reframing operations replace the composition). Also note: DEPTH-FG-FRAME overlaps with this category — pick one or the other.

---

## FRAME-DOORWAY — Doorway

Recompose this scene as viewed through a doorway. The door frame creates a natural border, dark edges framing the lit scene beyond. Adds mystery and depth.

---

## FRAME-WINDOW — Window

Recompose this scene as viewed through a window. Window frame and mullions create geometric framing. Perhaps reflections on the glass, or condensation. Indoor/outdoor boundary.

---

## FRAME-ARCH — Archway

Recompose this scene as viewed through an architectural archway. The curved arch creates an elegant natural frame. Stone or brick texture on the arch, scene revealed beyond.

---

## FRAME-FOLIAGE — Natural Foliage

Frame this scene with natural foliage. Leaves, branches, and vegetation create an organic border around the edges. Dappled light filtering through. Nature's own picture frame.

---

## FRAME-MIRROR — Mirror

Show this scene reflected in a mirror. The mirror's frame becomes the picture's frame. Adds depth, shows the space from a new angle, perhaps reveals what's behind the camera.

---

## FRAME-SILHOUETTE — Silhouette Foreground

Frame this scene with a dark silhouette in the foreground. A person, object, or architectural element in shadow creates a dramatic frame around the lit subject beyond.

---

## FRAME-VEHICLE — From Inside Vehicle

Frame this scene as viewed from inside a vehicle. Car window, rearview mirror, or windshield creates the frame. Dashboard or interior elements visible. Road trip perspective.

---

## FRAME-HANDS — Hand Rectangle

Frame this scene with hands forming a rectangle or heart shape. The classic director's framing gesture, or photographer composing a shot. Intimate, personal perspective.
