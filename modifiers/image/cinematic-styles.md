# Cinematic Styles

PURPOSE: defines the overall genre/mood look via color grading, contrast, and atmosphere
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants a named film/genre aesthetic (noir, technicolor, A24, horror, etc.)
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, weather, depth, framing, emotions, lenses, realism
CONFLICTS WITH: film-stocks, commercial-styles, photo-effects, cameras (all compete for the overall look)

---

## STYLE-PASTEL-SYMMETRY — Pastel Symmetrical

Change the image to have pastel color grading, perfectly symmetrical composition, centered framing, soft pink and mint tones, whimsical atmosphere, meticulous production design, storybook aesthetic.

---

## STYLE-NEON-NOIR — Neon Noir

Change the image to have neon noir aesthetic, vibrant pink and cyan lighting, rain-slicked surfaces, strong atmospheric haze, cyberpunk color palette, high contrast shadows, reflective wet streets.

---

## STYLE-TEAL-ORANGE — Teal And Orange

Change the image to have teal-orange color grade, strong complementary color contrast, warm skin tones, cool shadows, blockbuster film look, dramatic separation, modern Hollywood aesthetic.

---

## STYLE-DESAT-GRITTY — Desaturated Gritty War

Change the image to have desaturated gritty look, muted colors, greenish-gray tones, harsh contrast, documentary realism, war film aesthetic, bleach bypass effect, intense mood.

---

## STYLE-SEPIA — Vintage Sepia

Change the image to vintage sepia-toned aesthetic. Warm brown color cast, faded blacks, soft vignette. Old photograph quality, nostalgic antique feel. Slightly reduced sharpness, aged paper texture.

---

## STYLE-NORDIC-NOIR — Nordic Noir

Change the image to Nordic noir aesthetic. Cold desaturated blues and grays, overcast flat lighting. Moody Scandinavian crime drama look. Muted colors, melancholic atmosphere, stark minimalism.

---

## STYLE-HOLLYWOOD-GLAMOUR — Golden Age Hollywood

Change the image to Golden Age Hollywood glamour. High contrast black and white, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting. Soft focus glow on skin, theatrical staging. 1940s film noir elegance.

---

## STYLE-TECHNICOLOR — Technicolor

Change the image to Technicolor aesthetic. Hyper-saturated primary colors, especially reds and greens. High contrast, vivid unnatural color. 1950s musical film vibrancy, theatrical color.

---

## STYLE-INDIE-A24 — Indie A24

Change the image to indie film aesthetic. Visible film grain, slightly underexposed. Muted earth tones, natural available light feel. Handheld intimacy, raw authentic quality. A24 style.

---

## STYLE-SCIFI-BLUE — Sci-Fi Blue

Change the image to sci-fi blue aesthetic. Cool blue color cast throughout, high contrast. Sterile futuristic lighting, clinical atmosphere. Technology-forward, cold metallic feel.

---

## STYLE-HORROR — Horror

Change the image to horror film aesthetic. Heavily desaturated with sickly green undertones. High contrast, crushed shadows hiding details. Unsettling color grade, dread atmosphere.

---

## STYLE-ROMANTIC-GLOW — Romantic Soft Glow

Change the image to romantic soft glow aesthetic. Warm diffused lighting, soft halation on highlights. Dreamy pastel tones, gentle contrast. Ethereal romantic atmosphere, flattering skin.
