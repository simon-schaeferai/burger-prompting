# Angles

PURPOSE: controls camera position and orientation relative to the subject
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants to change the camera's vantage point, perspective, or tilt
STACKS WITH: shots, lenses, lighting, film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, depth, framing, realism, emotions, weather
CONFLICTS WITH: single-shots (reframing operations override angle choice)

---

## ANGLE-EYE — Eye Level

Change the camera angle to eye level. Camera at subject's natural height, neutral and balanced perspective.

---

## ANGLE-BIRDS-EYE — Bird's Eye View

Change the camera angle to a bird's eye view. Camera directly overhead at 90 degrees looking straight down. Top-down perspective.

---

## ANGLE-OTS — Over-The-Shoulder

Change the camera angle to an over-the-shoulder shot. Something in soft foreground, main subject sharp in background.

---

## ANGLE-THREE-QUARTER — Three-Quarter Angle

Change the camera angle to a three-quarter angle. Camera 45 degrees to subject. Shows depth and dimension.

---

## ANGLE-LOW — Low Angle

Change the camera angle to a low angle. Camera below subject looking up. Subject appears larger, more dominant.

---

## ANGLE-WORMS-EYE — Worm's Eye View

Change the camera angle to a worm's eye view. Camera on ground looking straight up. Extreme dramatic perspective.

---

## ANGLE-POV — Point Of View

Change the camera angle to a point-of-view shot. Camera shows the perspective from the subject's viewpoint. First-person, immersive.

---

## ANGLE-FRONTAL — Frontal Angle

Change the camera angle to a frontal angle. Camera directly facing subject. Symmetrical, direct confrontation.

---

## ANGLE-HIGH — High Angle

Change the camera angle to a high angle. Camera above subject looking down. Subject appears smaller, overview perspective.

---

## ANGLE-DUTCH — Dutch Angle

Change the camera angle to a dutch angle. Camera tilted 15-30 degrees, diagonal horizon. Creates tension and unease.

---

## ANGLE-PROFILE — Profile Angle

Change the camera angle to a profile angle. Camera at 90 degrees to subject. Pure side view, silhouette potential.

---

## ANGLE-BEHIND — Shot From Behind

Change the camera angle to a shot from behind. Camera behind subject showing what lies ahead. Mystery, anticipation.
