# Realism

PURPOSE: boosts perceived realism (detail, imperfections, physics-based depth, photoreal quality)
WHEN TO LOAD: when the user wants the output to look more like a real photograph and less like a render or illustration
STACKS WITH: shots, angles, lighting, film-stocks, cinematic-styles, commercial-styles, depth, framing, lenses, emotions, weather, cameras
CONFLICTS WITH: creative (which intentionally bends reality), and some photo-effects that push the image toward stylized/unreal looks (EFFECT-DUOTONE, EFFECT-INFRARED, EFFECT-CYANOTYPE, EFFECT-TILT-SHIFT)

---

## REALISM-HYPER-DETAIL — Hyper-Realistic Detail

Add hyper-realistic fine details throughout this image. Enhance surface textures, micro-details, and subtle variations. Skin shows pores and fine lines. Materials show weave, grain, or wear. Everything gains tactile, believable detail visible at close inspection.

---

## REALISM-IMPERFECTIONS — Natural Imperfections

Make this more realistic by adding natural imperfections. Nothing too perfect or artificial. Add subtle wear, asymmetries, and organic irregularities appropriate to whatever is in the scene. Authentic, unretouched quality.

---

## REALISM-3D-DEPTH — Three-Dimensional Depth

Enhance three-dimensional depth. Stronger atmospheric perspective, distant elements slightly hazier. More pronounced shadows defining form. Better separation between foreground and background. Everything feels solid in real space.

---

## REALISM-PHOTOREAL — Photorealistic

Transform to photorealistic quality indistinguishable from a real photograph. Accurate physics-based lighting, proper shadows and reflections. Natural colors, realistic lens characteristics. Remove anything that looks artificial or CGI.

---

## REALISM-AMBIENT — Ambient Atmosphere

Enrich with realistic ambient atmosphere. Dust motes in light, subtle haze, environmental particles. Background feels alive with small details. The scene breathes and exists beyond the main subject.

---

## REALISM-DOF — Shallow Depth Of Field

Change the depth of field to blur the background. Subject stays sharp and in focus. Background becomes soft and out of focus with creamy bokeh. Strong separation between subject and background. Shallow depth of field effect.
